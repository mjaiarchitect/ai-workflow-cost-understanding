I own the company.
300 people. One AI bill.

A comic of me (the boss) trying to give every employee research AI + desktop AI — and learning that “$20 seats” is not the same as “enough tokens.”

Me · Owner Team · 300
Episode 01

My plant never sleeps

~100 on shift

“I run a company with 300 employees. Three shifts. About 100 people on at once. Every one needs AI — planning (Kimi K3) and desktop automation (UI-Mate).”

24×7 coverage · 365 days · two models · five-year horizon. Same story math: ceil(300÷3) = 100 on shift.

300 roster 100 concurrent K3 + UI-Mate
Episode 02

The “cheap” plan

$20 seat · quota done

“Spreadsheet says $20 × 300 = $6,000 / month. Looks fine. Buy the seats!”

“Boss… my quota died in two days. I can’t research. The GUI agent stopped mid-click.”

The seat was a money unit, not a promise of continuous tokens. Work stalled. I still paid $6k — and got blocked.

Quota ≠ 24×7 capacity Need enough tokens / month
Episode 03

I multiply wrong… then right

100 × 100= 10,000 tok/s

“If each of my 100 on-shift people needs 100 tok/s… that’s not one shared 100. That’s 10,000 tok/s per model!”

Wrong: 100 tok/s × 86,400 s → ~260 M / mo Right: 100 × 100 = 10,000 tok/s Month: 10,000 × 2,628,000 = ~26.3 B tokens …and I need that for K3 AND UI-Mate
Flaw: understated ~100× Floor: 26.3 B + 26.3 B
Episode 04

One cute rack is not enough

1 nodeneed 41 + 72

“Can one node even load Kimi K3? Yes — 8× B300-class. Can one node spit 10,000 tok/s? Nope.”

Mid lab: K3 244 tok/s → ~641 M / mo ✗ ~41× short UI 140 tok/s → ~368 M / mo ✗ ~71× short ceil(10,000/244)= 41 K3 nodes ceil(10,000/140)= 72 UI nodes
1+1 ≈ 1% of peak Peak mid: 41 + 72
Episode 05

Not everyone generates at once

1 of 5= ~20% AI

“Peak assumes all 100 people decode at once, forever. Real work is bursty — type, wait, read, click, think.”

“So I add a queue / scheduler. Size for ~20 active streams, not 100. Users still feel snappy.”

Peak: 10,000 tok/s → 41 + 72 nodes Queue ~20%: 2,000 tok/s → 9 + 15 nodes Tokens / mo / model → ~5.3 B (not 26.3 B)

Episode 08

What I tape on my wall

lesson!

“First fill employees × tok/s. Then choose peak insurance vs a shared queue. Then pick the buy path.”

Own-buy still wins on dollars if I accept ops risk (power, parts past 3-year warranty, software queue). Hosted ≈ 10×. Enough seats ≈ 14–15×. The old $6k Pro seats? Cute comic — not capacity.
